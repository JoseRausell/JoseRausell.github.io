About

I hold a PhD in Telecommunications from the Universitat Politècnica de València (2026), with the thesis Integrated Programmable Photonics for Unconventional Computing Applications. I am now a postdoctoral researcher at the Photonics Research Labs (ITEAM, UPV), and I also hold a Master's in Artificial Intelligence from the Universitat de València.

My research focuses on programmable photonic integrated circuits for computing: how to design them and how to control them, including machine-learning methods that calibrate and program analog photonic processors. I am also interested in using neural networks for electromagnetic simulation, such as neural operators that act as fast surrogates for full-wave solvers.

    Projects

    Programmable Photonics · Interactive Lab

    A set of in-browser simulators for the building blocks of programmable photonics: MZIs, rings, add-drop filters, couplers, MMIs, thermo-optic phase shifters and Reck/Clements meshes. Built from analytical and transfer-matrix models for teaching.

    Neural operators for electromagnetic scattering

    Master's thesis comparing FNO, F-FNO, UNO, CNO and U-Net as surrogates for FDTD. The F-FNO reaches a 1.95% relative L² error with sub-millisecond inference, versus about 8.7 s per Meep simulation, and the operators keep working at unseen resolutions.
